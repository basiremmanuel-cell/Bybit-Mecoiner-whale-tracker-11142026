BYBIT MEMECOIN WHALE TRACKER
============================

CONTENTS
- index.html: the complete tracker dashboard
- README.txt: installation guide

ANDROID CHROME INSTALLATION
1. Download the ZIP file to your Android phone.
2. Open Files / My Files > Downloads.
3. Tap the ZIP and choose Extract. If your phone has no Extract option,
   install ZArchiver from Google Play, open it, locate the ZIP, tap it,
   and choose Extract here.
4. Open the extracted folder.
5. Tap index.html and choose Chrome.
6. Bookmark the page in Chrome for quick access.

IMPORTANT
- This is a no-API, no-wallet-connection research dashboard.
- It does not fetch live Bybit prices or automatically discover tokens.
- Open the three research websites, inspect the same token, and enter
  your verified scores manually.
- Saved watchlist reviews stay in that browser's local storage. Clearing
  Chrome site data may erase them.
- Confirm the exact spot pair is listed and tradable on Bybit before
  making any decision.
- Scores are a checklist, not financial advice or a profit guarantee.

DESKTOP
Extract the ZIP and double-click index.html to open it in a modern browser.
